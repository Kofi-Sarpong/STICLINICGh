# User Persona

## Kwame

**Profile:** Third-year University of Ghana student

### Scenario

Kwame experiences concerning symptoms and visits a local clinic for a checkup. His test results indicate a positive HIV status.

Faced with denial, confusion, and concern about stigma within his university community, he seeks a second opinion and comprehensive services. He searches online for alternative facilities but struggles to find adequate information about available services and nearby clinics.

### Key Challenges

- Concern about HIV-related stigma
- Need for accurate and understandable information
- Difficulty identifying suitable healthcare facilities
- Desire for confidentiality
- Need for counselling and support
- Need for a straightforward way to schedule care

### How STICLINICGh Addresses These Needs

- HIV/STI information
- Directory of relevant clinics
- Online counselling
- Appointment booking
- A more integrated pathway to sexual-health services
